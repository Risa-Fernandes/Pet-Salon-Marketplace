About Pawfect Care 🐾

Pawfect Care is a modern pet-care platform designed to provide smart healthcare, grooming, accessories, and AI-powered support for pet owners through one easy-to-use system.

Who We Are

Pawfect Care was created with a simple vision — making pet care easier, smarter, and more accessible for every pet owner. Our platform combines veterinary services, pet salons, pet accessories, and AI health assistance into one modern digital experience.

We understand that pets are family members, and caring for them should never feel complicated. That is why Pawfect Care focuses on providing trusted services, user-friendly technology, and a smooth experience for users.

Built using Flask, SQLAlchemy, Bootstrap, Tailwind CSS, and modern web technologies, Pawfect Care offers a responsive, secure, and premium platform for both pet owners and service providers.
