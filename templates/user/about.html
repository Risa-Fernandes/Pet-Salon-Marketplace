About Pawfect Care 🐾

A Full-Stack Pet Care Management Platform

Our Mission

Pawfect Care is a full-stack web application developed by a team of four developers. Our mission is to simplify pet care services by connecting pet owners with trusted salons, veterinarians, and service providers through a secure and user-friendly digital platform.

The frontend team focused on responsive UI design and smooth user experience, while the backend team developed REST APIs using Flask, handled authentication, and managed database integration for efficient service management.
